# Two Ways

**The opening lecture says the West chose the lesser of two questions.**

> "there are two fundamental ways of looking at the world."
>
> [1. What Is, What Should Be](lectures/1-what-is-what-should-be.md).

> "I think we picked the wrong one."
>
> [1. What Is, What Should Be](lectures/1-what-is-what-should-be.md).

The two ways, as he spells them out, are what the world is made of, and how to act in it. The first can be asked from experience or as a materialist. The second is the one he thinks should have been primary.

> "figuring out how to act in the world is arguably of primary importance"
>
> [1. What Is, What Should Be](lectures/1-what-is-what-should-be.md).

He adds that it is perhaps of primary reality. The title of the lecture is What Is, What Should Be. "What is" is the stuff question. "What should be" is the action question. The rest of the notebook is the second question, told as a story.
