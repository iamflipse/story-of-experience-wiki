# Glossary

His labels, as these lectures use them.

- **Two ways.** What the world is made of, and how to act in it. He thinks the second should be primary.
- **What beckons.** The question that starts the path, asked before you know who you are.
- **Point A, point B.** The basic story, plus the behaviors that turn one point into the other.
- **Meta-story.** Establishing a frame, then having that frame disrupted and rebuilt.
- **Yin and yang.** Serpents back to back. A center of a cloud of nearer and farther meanings.
- **Dragon.** A sign whose core is predation: uncertainty, danger, and opportunity. The unknown that order comes out of.
- **The patriarchy.** His one-word answer for what shields you from unmediated nature. The following lines say: you, society, nature, and clothing as the layer between a body and the world.
- **Hierarchy of being.** Attention stacked, as in keys under words under sentences under paragraphs.
- **Marduk's fifty names.** One high god as an amalgam of older tribal gods.
- **Sacrifice.** The cost in the story, sharpest when it falls on a child and on the parent who bears that.
