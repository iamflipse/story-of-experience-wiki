# The Path

**A life, in these lectures, is a path, and the path starts with a question you cannot answer by résumé.**

> "What beckons?"
>
> [2. Pathways and Obstacles](lectures/2-pathways-and-obstacles.md).

He calls that a spiritual journey and says it starts in humility.

> "you don't know who you are, seriously, just like you don't know who anyone else is."
>
> [2. Pathways and Obstacles](lectures/2-pathways-and-obstacles.md).

From there the work is listening, investigating, and negotiating, including with the question of whether you even want to be satisfied.

Later, the meta-narrative lecture names the frame those questions sit in.

> "the basic story, point A, point B, and the behaviors necessary to transform one into the other."
>
> [7. The Meta-Narrative of Transformation](lectures/7-the-meta-narrative-of-transformation.md).

He says the lectures before that one had already set this frame, then moved to how a simple story gets disrupted, then to a meta-story: the establishing of a story, a game, a frame, and the disruption of that frame. Point A to point B is the small story. The course, as he describes it there, is also the story of how those stories break and get rebuilt.
