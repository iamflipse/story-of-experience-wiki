# Hierarchies

**Hierarchies of Being starts with a person at a keyboard, not with a cosmic chart.**

> "When I lived in Montreal in the 1980s, I was typing away on my computer."
>
> [5. Hierarchies of Being](lectures/5-hierarchies-of-being.md).

He was aware of the keys, though he says he was already an accomplished typist, so most of the attention was on the words, the screen, a focal point. Attention did not stay on one rung.

> "Then I'd bounce up and down from word and phrase to sentence and to paragraph."
>
> [5. Hierarchies of Being](lectures/5-hierarchies-of-being.md).

That bounce is the hierarchy: key, word, phrase, sentence, paragraph, each one a tool for the one above it and a world for the one below. The lecture's title is the claim that being is arranged this way, and the typing story is the ordinary case.
