# Balance

**Sacred Balance treats an old diagram as a tool, not as a decoration.**

> "Those are serpents back to back, by the way, the yin and yang symbol."
>
> [3. Sacred Balance](lectures/3-sacred-balance.md).

He says the figure has lasted because it is absurdly useful. Yang and yin sit in a cloud of meaning. Each word, he says, occupies the center of a cloud: denotations close in, connotations farther out, the way a visual field shades off. The symbol is one center. The lecture walks the cloud.

He does not give a one-line slogan that exhausts the symbol. The claim to keep is that the pair is serpents set against each other, and that the usefulness is the reason it survived.
